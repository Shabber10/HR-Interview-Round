## 🌟 Answer Design Principles
- **No Complex Jargon**: Replaced dense corporate buzzwords with natural spoken language.
- **Clear & Strong**: Gets straight to the point with high confidence and structured logic.
- **Realistic Scenarios**: Real-world examples of bugs, deadlines, teamwork, and decision-making.
